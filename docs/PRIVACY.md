# DUST & DEAD: Privacy Policy (DRAFT, not yet published)
_Last updated: DRAFT_

DUST & DEAD is a free game. We don't require an account, and we don't show ads or use analytics or tracking SDKs.

**What we collect**
- **Leaderboard (optional):** if you post a score, we store the nickname you type plus your round, points and kills on our leaderboard server.
- **Co-op (optional):** while you play co-op, our game server receives your nickname, in-game position, aim and shots so it can run the match. Matches are held in memory and discarded when the room ends. Server logs may briefly contain your IP address for abuse prevention.
- **On your device:** settings (sound, graphics, map, mode) and your local best scores are saved in your browser's storage.

**What we don't do:** we don't sell or share data, collect contact details, location, contacts or device identifiers, or knowingly collect data from children under 13.
